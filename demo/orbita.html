MY BODY

Understand each system.

What we measure, what we can observe and what we still do not know.

01 / CARDIOVASCULAR

Pulse and pressure

Tracking lets you observe changes. Activity, symptoms and method help interpret them. A daily entry is not equivalent to an ECG and does not assess thrombosis.

02 / REST AND WELLBEING

How you slept and how you feel

Self-report adds context. Perceived hours of sleep do not determine REM/NREM stages. This profile has no rest series yet.

03 / PHYSICAL FUNCTION

Muscles, bones and physical capacity

Strength, aerobic capacity and DXA tests require specific equipment and protocols. They are not deduced from pulse or body mass.

Not assessed in this demo
04 / INFORMATION QUALITY

The unknown counts too

An area without measurements keeps the "not assessed" state. The body outline organizes information and does not identify where a disease is located.
