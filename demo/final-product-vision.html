    What was your change associated with?

    Comparison within your own history, between tests done with the same method.

    Why there is no "winning routine"

    • 1In NASA's Sprint study, high-intensity training did not outperform the usual routine on VO2peak. Participants chose their group (A02).
    • 2Among 46 astronauts there was no no-exercise group: how much capacity would have changed without it is unknown (A03).
    • 3Capacity tends to drop early in flight and recover over the days, whatever each person does (B19). A change between tests mixes exercise and adaptation.
    • 4Missing intensity is not counted as low intensity: it stays not recorded (B24).
